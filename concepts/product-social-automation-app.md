# Social Media Automation App

**Idea**: Create an app similar to existing Instagram automation apps (which auto-respond to comments and DMs).

**Go-to-Market**: 
- Launch on Product Hunt.
- Make it cheaper than competitors.

**Features**:
- Auto-posting every day on Instagram, LinkedIn, Reddit, Facebook (account, page, groups), and Twitter.
- Auto-replying on Twitter and Reddit.
