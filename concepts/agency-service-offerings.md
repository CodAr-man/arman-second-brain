# Agency Service Offerings

The "Complete Brand Strategy" services offered by [[bluesmithera-ai]]. The goal is Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).

## The Complete Brand Strategy
- **Website Development**: Design/redesign websites from scratch. For SMBs, the website will act as an ERP/CRM/CMS (handle customer relationships, payments, analytics, billing).
- **Social Media Management**: Set up WhatsApp Business API. Auto-post updates from website directly to socials.
- **SEO & AEO**: Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, Bing Webmaster Tools, Google Search Console, keyword optimization, backlink building. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) for ChatGPT and Claude recommendations.
- **Google Business Profile setup**.
- **Automations**: Review business and suggest suitable automations.
- **Content & Ads**: Lead generation, email outreach strategy, blog writing, Google ads.
