# ERP Outreach Strategy

A targeted cold email outreach strategy designed to help businesses fix or improve their ERP systems.

**Target Audiences & Offers:**
1. **Odoo Users:** Target businesses currently using Odoo and offer them a custom Odoo solution or development.
2. **General ERP Struggles:** Find businesses facing problems with their current ERP systems. 
   - **Offer:** A free audit on their ERP system to solve issues their ERP cannot handle out-of-the-box.
   - **Upsell/Solution:** Solve these issues through custom development, automation, or AI integration.
