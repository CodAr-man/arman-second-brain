# Data Scraping & Selling Strategy

Similar to how businesses sell databases on IndiaMart, you can build your own proprietary databases by scraping highly targeted audience data and selling it to the relevant industry.

**The Workflow:**
1. Use Apify scrapers to extract data from relevant platforms (e.g., YouTube commenters, channel subscribers, Twitter followers).
2. Segment the data based on the niche (e.g., credit card enthusiasts, trading geeks).
3. Sell the database as warm/targeted leads to B2B companies in that space.

**Examples:**
- Scrape viewers of credit card review YouTube channels $\rightarrow$ Sell leads to Credit Card Companies.
- Scrape viewers of finance/trading channels (like Rachana Ranade) $\rightarrow$ Sell leads to Trading Firms/Brokerages.
- Scrape viewers of real estate channels $\rightarrow$ Sell leads to Real Estate Developers.
