# Free Market Research (The Whitepaper Hack)

Major consulting firms like [[McKinsey]], [[BCG]], and [[PwC]] spend hundreds of thousands of dollars researching global trends, emerging industries, and disruptive tech (e.g., AI, Renewable Energy, Biotech, Fintech).

They post this research for free on their websites because they are selling multi-million dollar consulting contracts to Fortune 500 corporations, not to small entrepreneurs.

**The Strategy:**
1. Do not pay for market research. Steal it.
2. Go to their websites and download their free whitepapers.
3. See what industries they say are being disrupted or what problems are emerging.
4. Build solutions around those problems *before* the wave peaks.

Corporations read the reports to make billion-dollar decisions; smart entrepreneurs act on them to build multi-million dollar solutions. The opportunity isn't in the data, it's in the execution.
