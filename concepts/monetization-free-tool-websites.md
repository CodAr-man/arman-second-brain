# Monetization via Free Tool Websites

**Idea**: Launch free tool-based websites.

**Execution**:
- Find completely open-source repos.
- Turn them into user-friendly websites.
- Monetize through Ads or Subscriptions.

Also related: Directory-based websites (e.g., building an Airbnb-like site from a directory).
